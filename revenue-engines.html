Revenue Engines

Transform your car park into a multi-service mobility hub.

Mist Wheels Car Wash

How it works

An eco-friendly, waterless car washing service operated directly within the parking facility. Payments are processed through the central parking platform.

Driver Benefit

Vehicles are cleaned professionally while the owner works or shops, saving valuable time.

Property Owner Benefit

Unlocks a new revenue stream without sacrificing parking bays to bulky wet-wash equipment.

Investment & Operations

SkyVora can fund the equipment and provide trained staffing under a revenue-share model.

Motor Insurance Renewal

How it works

Digital kiosks and payment stations integrate with third-party insurance providers to offer instant policy renewals.

Driver Benefit

A highly convenient, trusted touchpoint to renew mandatory insurance while paying for parking.

Property Owner Benefit

Generates commission-based revenue from foot traffic passing through payment points.

Investment & Operations

We provide the digital interfaces and manage the third-party API integrations.

Transparency

Revenue shares are automatically calculated and reported in the central ledger.

EV Charging

How it works

Installation of smart electric vehicle charging infrastructure tied into the parking management system.

Driver Benefit

Safe, reliable charging using the same cashless payment methods they use for parking.

Property Owner Benefit

Attracts high-value patrons, future-proofs the asset, and generates electricity markup revenue.

Investment & Operations

We supply the hardware and manage the metering and maintenance.

Transparency

Power consumption and charging revenue are detailed in the unified client portal.
