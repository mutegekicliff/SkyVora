Who We Are

About SkyVora

Pioneering smart facility solutions across two core pillars: intelligent, zero-leakage parking management and eco-friendly waterless car washing.

Transforming Parking Facilities into Multi-Service Hubs

SkyVora is a smart-mobility and facility technology company operating across East and Southern Africa. We bridge the gap between world-class automation and local operating realities by excelling in two synergistic business lines: intelligent parking and access control, and on-demand waterless car washing.

Whether deploying offline-resilient access infrastructure or setting up eco-friendly car care within parking facilities, we empower commercial property owners, shopping malls, and transport hubs to maximize efficiency, stop revenue leakage, and generate high-margin secondary income.

Regional Presence & Local Capability

With corporate headquarters in Uganda and active regional operations in Zambia and Kenya, SkyVora provides end-to-end design, equipment supply, on-site operations, and responsive 24/7 technical support.

Core Pillars

Our Two Core Lines of Business

We combine cutting-edge technology and sustainable on-site services to create seamless experiences for drivers and profitable operations for property owners.

Pillar 1: Technology & Operations

Intelligent Parking & Access Management

We design, install, operate, and maintain fully automated parking and revenue management systems engineered specifically for African power and network conditions.

  • Ticketless & Cashless Entry: ANPR license plate recognition, QR codes, and seamless Mobile Money integration (M-Pesa, Airtel, MTN).
  • Offline Resilience: Standby Box hardware architecture guaranteeing 100% operational continuity during internet outages.
  • Zero Revenue Leakage: Real-time cloud telemetry and automated audit dashboards for total financial transparency.
Explore Parking Solutions
Pillar 2: Sustainable Services

Waterless Car Washing (Mist Wheels)

Our innovative waterless, mist-based vehicle care system operates directly inside parking facilities—turning idle vehicle dwell time into a premium, eco-friendly driver service.

  • Water Conservation: Saves up to 200 liters of water per vehicle wash using advanced biodegradable misting emulsions.
  • Zero Facility Disruption: No messy water runoff, drainage infrastructure, or sacrificed parking bays required.
  • New Revenue Generation: Shared revenue models for property owners with service ordering integrated into the parking flow.
