About Us

Designing, installing, and operating intelligent parking systems across Africa and the Middle East.

Company Overview

SkyVora is a parking technology and operations company. We specialise in designing, installing, operating, and maintaining intelligent parking, access-control, and traffic-management systems across Africa and the Middle East.

We are the authorised distributor and systems integrator for Cytel (Shanghai) Ltd and its acclaimed Minicloud parking platform. Our expertise bridges the gap between global technology and the realities of African operating conditions.

Regional Growth Strategy

With headquarters in Uganda and active operations spanning Zambia and Kenya, we maintain robust local capability. Our regional approach ensures rapid deployment and dedicated technical support right where our clients need it.

Our Vision

Our Mission
